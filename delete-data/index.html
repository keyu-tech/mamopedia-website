    Delete Your Data

    You can delete the data Mamopedia has stored for you at any time from inside the app. Here is how it works, step by step.

    1. 1

      Log in to your account

      Open the Mamopedia app and sign in with the account whose data you want to delete.

    2. 2

      Go to the app settings

      Open the settings screen from the app menu.

    3. 3

      Click on "Delete data"

      Find the "Delete data" option in settings and select it.

    4. 4

      Confirm the deletion

      Confirm your choice when the app asks you to. This starts the deletion.

    5. 5

      We notify you when it's done

      The deletion process starts in the background and you will be notified once it is done.

    notifications_active

    Your account stays active

    Deleting your data clears what Mamopedia has stored for you - you can keep using the app and start fresh.

    If you can't access the app or need help, email us at info@keyu.tech. To remove your account entirely, see Delete Your Account.
