    Delete Your Account

    You can delete your Mamopedia account at any time from inside the app. Here is how it works, step by step.

    1. 1

      Log in to your account

      Open the Mamopedia app and sign in with the account you want to delete.

    2. 2

      Go to the app settings

      Open the settings screen from the app menu.

    3. 3

      Click on "Delete account"

      Find the "Delete account" option in settings and select it.

    4. 4

      Confirm the deletion

      Confirm your choice when the app asks you to. This starts the deletion.

    5. 5

      Your account is deleted after 15 days

      Your account will be deleted after 15 days if you don't log in again.

    schedule

    Changed your mind?

    Logging back in during those 15 days cancels the deletion and keeps your account active.

    If you can't access the app or need help, email us at info@keyu.tech. To delete only your data and keep your account, see Delete Your Data.
